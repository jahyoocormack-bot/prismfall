ROYALE

Glide in, grab weapons from the glowing beams, and stay inside the white circle. The purple storm shrinks the arena and burns through health directly. Last one standing is the Prism Champion.

DEATHMATCH

Everyone respawns. Pickups on the arena floor come back after a few seconds. First to 20 eliminations — or the leader when the clock runs out — wins.

LOOT

Common · Rare · Epic · Legendary — higher rarity hits harder. Blue cells charge your shield, pink kits heal, green pads launch you skyward.

NO MOUSE CAPTURE?

If a site or embedded window blocks mouse capture, the game switches to free-look by itself: move the pointer to look and rest it near a screen edge to keep turning.

TOUCH SCREENS

Left thumb moves, drag the right side to look, and use the on-screen buttons to fire, aim, jump, slide and swap. Push the stick all the way forward to sprint.

PROGRESS

Every match pays XP and coins. Spend coins in the Locker on weapon wraps and tracer rounds.
